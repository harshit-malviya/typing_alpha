# Training Modes, Visual Aids & Customization Guide

Beyond the day-by-day **Learning Calendar**, TypingAlpha includes **6 dedicated training and analytics views** in the left sidebar so you can practice any skill on demand.

---

## 1. Touch-Typing Course (6 Modules, 24 Lessons)
A structured, self-paced curriculum organized into 6 modules:
- **Module 1 — Home Row Foundation**: `F & J`, `D & K`, `S & L`, `A & ;`, `G & H`
- **Module 2 — Top Row Reach**: `E & I`, `R & U`, `T & Y`, `W & O`, `Q & P`
- **Module 3 — Bottom Row & Full Alphabet**: `V & M`, `B & N`, `C & ,`, `X & .`, `Z & /` (plus pangrams)
- **Module 4 — Capitalization & Opposite-Hand Shift**: Left Shift with right-hand capitals, Right Shift with left-hand capitals, and dialogue punctuation (`' " ? !`)
- **Module 5 — Numbers & Symbols**: Left-hand numbers (`1–5`), right-hand numbers (`6–0`), and brackets/symbols (`@ # $ % & ( ) [ ]`)
- **Module 6 — N-Gram Fluency & Speed Mastery**: High-frequency bigrams (`th, he, in, er`), trigrams (`-ing, -tion, -ment`), and full paragraph graduation

Each lesson grades your performance from **1 to 3 Stars** (`★`) based on net WPM and accuracy thresholds.

---

## 2. Adaptive Weak-Key Practice (Keybr-Style)
- Starts with the 6 most common letters in English (`E, N, I, T, R, L`) and tracks your per-key speed and accuracy confidence (`0%` to `100%`).
- Every generated drill focuses automatically on your **weakest unlocked key** (highlighted with a golden border in the alphabet bar).
- As soon as your confidence on the active key reaches **$\ge 80\%$**, the next letter in frequency order unlocks automatically (or you can click `+1 Key` / `Unlock All 26` manually at any time).
- Includes optional toggles for **Capitals** and **Punctuation**.

---

## 3. Speed & N-Gram Tests (Monkeytype-Style)
Test and push your raw speed across customizable configurations:
- **Word Count Mode**: `15`, `25`, `50`, or `100` words
- **Timed Mode**: `15s`, `30s`, `60s`, or `120s` countdown tests
- **5 Specialized Word Pools**:
  1. `English 200` (Top 200 most frequent words)
  2. `English 1000` (Extended everyday vocabulary)
  3. `Top Bigrams` (Words packed with `th, he, in, er, an, re, on`)
  4. `Top Trigrams` (Words packed with `the, and, ing, ion, ent, for`)
  5. `Alternating Hands` (Words that alternate rapidly between left and right hands for peak rhythm)
- Optional **@ Punctuation** and **# Numbers** modifiers.

---

## 4. Quotes, Literature & Programming Code Snippets
- **Famous Quotes & Book Excerpts**: Practice natural sentence flow, capital letters, commas, and quotation marks with curated excerpts from Marcus Aurelius, Richard Feynman, Cal Newport, James Clear, Carl Sagan, J.R.R. Tolkien, Linus Torvalds, and Seneca.
- **Programming Code Snippets**: Practice typing indentation, brackets, parentheses, colons, and operators in **Python**, **TypeScript**, **C++**, and **Rust**.

---

## 5. Custom Text Studio
- Click **Edit Custom Text** to paste any article, study notes, or book chapter you want to practice.
- In the Windows desktop app, click **Load Local .TXT File** to open any `.txt`, `.md`, `.py`, `.js`, `.ts`, `.cpp`, or `.rs` file directly from your computer.

---

## 6. Visual Learning Aids, Audio & Themes
- **Interactive On-Screen Keyboard**: Color-coded by finger zone (`Pinky`, `Ring`, `Middle`, `L-Index`, `R-Index`, `Thumbs`). Automatically highlights both the next target key and the **opposite-hand `Shift` key** when typing uppercase letters or symbols.
- **Dual-Hand SVG Finger Guide**: Displays Left and Right hands alongside a central callout telling you which exact finger to press.
- **Keyboard Layouts**: Switch between **US QWERTY**, **UK QWERTY**, **Dvorak**, and **Colemak** in the top bar.
- **Mechanical Switch Sound Synthesizer**: Built-in Web Audio API synthesizer offering **Cherry MX Blue (Clicky)**, **Cherry MX Brown (Tactile)**, **Cherry MX Red (Linear Thock)**, **Vintage Typewriter**, or **Muted**.
- **5 Predefined Themes + Live Theme Studio (Inherit & Customize)**:
  - Switch instantly between `Dark Slate`, `Serika Dark`, `Cyberpunk Neon`, `Catppuccin Mocha`, and `Light Paper`.
  - Click **Customize Theme** in the top bar to open the **Theme Studio**: inherit base colors from any predefined theme, customize the **Smart 8-Color Palette** with real-time live preview across the entire app, and click **Save as New Theme Copy** (or export/import Theme JSON).
- **Smooth Caret & Zen Mode**: Choose between `Smooth Line (|)`, `Solid Block (█)`, or `Underline (_)` carets and toggle **Zen Focus Mode**.
