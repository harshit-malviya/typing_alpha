# Getting Started & Touch-Typing Ergonomics Guide

Welcome to **TypingAlpha** — a 100% free, offline, open-source touch-typing trainer for Windows. This guide walks you through installing the app, setting up proper hand posture, and completing your first guided workout.

---

## 1. Installing or Launching TypingAlpha on Windows

You can run TypingAlpha in three ways depending on your preference:

### Option A: Windows Setup Installer (Recommended)
1. Run **[TypingAlpha-Setup-1.0.0.exe](../release/TypingAlpha-Setup-1.0.0.exe)** inside the `release/` folder.
2. Choose your preferred installation folder and click **Install**.
3. Launch **TypingAlpha** anytime from your **Windows Desktop** shortcut or **Start Menu**.

### Option B: Standalone Portable Executable (No Installation)
1. Run **[TypingAlpha-Portable-1.0.0.exe](../release/TypingAlpha-Portable-1.0.0.exe)** directly from any folder or USB drive.
2. No installation wizard is required, and your progress is still saved automatically to `%APPDATA%\typing-alpha\user-progress.json`.

### Option C: Quick Developer / Source Launch
- Double-click **[Start-TypingAlpha.bat](../Start-TypingAlpha.bat)** in the project root, or run:
  ```powershell
  npm run electron:start
  ```

## 2. The Golden Rules of Touch-Typing Posture

Before pressing your first key, set up your physical workspace:

1. **Feel the Tactile Bumps on `F` and `J`**:
   - Look at your physical keyboard once to notice the small raised ridges on the **`F`** and **`J`** keys.
   - Place your **Left Index Finger** on **`F`** and your **Right Index Finger** on **`J`**.
2. **Rest All 8 Fingers on the Home Row**:
   - **Left Hand**: Pinky on **`A`**, Ring on **`S`**, Middle on **`D`**, Index on **`F`**.
   - **Right Hand**: Index on **`J`**, Middle on **`K`**, Ring on **`L`**, Pinky on **`;`**.
   - **Thumbs**: Rest both thumbs lightly on the **Spacebar**.
3. **Eyes on the Screen — Never on the Keyboard**:
   - Use TypingAlpha's **On-Screen Visual Keyboard** and **Dual-Hand SVG Finger Guide** at the bottom of the screen to see where every key is located without looking down at your physical hands.
4. **Prioritize Accuracy Over Speed First**:
   - Keep **Stop on Error** enabled during your first two weeks. Speed comes naturally once your fingers know the exact path to each key without hesitation.

## 3. Useful Keyboard Shortcuts While Practicing

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Esc`** | Immediately restart the current lesson or speed test |
| **`Enter`** *(when drill finishes)* | Advance to the next step in your Daily Workout or next lesson |
| **`Backspace`** | Clear a blocked error indicator or delete the previous character (in *Type Through* mode) |
