# Learning Calendar & Guided Daily Workout Guide

The **Learning Calendar** is the heart of TypingAlpha's beginner-to-mastery curriculum. Instead of leaving you wondering what to practice each day, it provides a structured day-by-day study roadmap with a **3-Step Guided Daily Workout**.

---

## 1. Choosing Your Study Track

At the top of the **Learning Calendar** tab, you can switch anytime between three study tracks (your progress in each track is saved independently):

| Track | Duration | Daily Commitment | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **14-Day Intensive Bootcamp** | 14 Days | ~25 mins/day | Learners who want to master the full keyboard rapidly in two focused weeks. |
| **30-Day Mastery Roadmap** *(Default)* | 30 Days | ~18 mins/day | The ideal balanced pace from Home Row basics (`F & J`) to `45+ WPM` paragraph fluency, with built-in milestone review days every 5th day. |
| **60-Day Gentle Habit Builder** | 60 Days | ~10 mins/day | Busy learners who want short, stress-free daily sessions with extra muscle-memory reinforcement. |

---

## 2. How the 3-Step Guided Daily Workout Works

Every day in your schedule consists of **3 structured tasks** that run as an automatic playlist:

1. **Step 1 — New Key Lesson**:
   - Introduces the day's target keys (e.g., `F & J` on Day 1, `D & K` on Day 2, `S & L` on Day 3) with explicit finger coaching tips.
2. **Step 2 — Adaptive Weak-Key Drill**:
   - Algorithmically generates practice combinations weighted toward whichever keys were slowest or had the most mistakes in Step 1.
3. **Step 3 — Daily Fluency & Rhythm Benchmark**:
   - Tests your ability to type smoothly at target WPM and accuracy using your learned keys.

> **Beginner-Safe Cumulative Key Guarantee**:  
> On **Day $X$**, Step 1, Step 2, and Step 3 are strictly restricted to **only the keys you have learned from Day 1 up to Day $X$**.  
> - On **Day 1**, all 3 steps only use `F`, `J`, and `Space`.  
> - On **Day 2**, all 3 steps only use `F`, `J`, `D`, `K`, and `Space`.  
> - On **Day 4+**, once `A, S, D, F, J, K, L, ;` are unlocked, Step 2 and Step 3 automatically form **real English words** (`sad`, `dad`, `fall`, `salad`, `flask`, `glass`...) using only your learned keys!

## 3. Dual Calendar Views & Flexible Progression

- **Day-by-Day Roadmap Grid (`Day 1` – `Day N`)**:
  - Grouped into **4 Phases**:
    1. *Phase 1: Home Row & Top Row Foundation*
    2. *Phase 2: Bottom Row & Full Alphabet Reach*
    3. *Phase 3: Capitalization, Numbers & Symbols*
    4. *Phase 4: N-Gram Speed & Real-World Fluency*
  - Each Day Card displays its focus keys, `0/3` to `3/3` task completion dots, and a green checkmark once finished.
  - **No Penalties for Missed Days**: If you miss a calendar day, your roadmap never locks or penalizes you—simply pick up right where you left off, replay past days, or work ahead whenever you want.
- **Monthly Activity Calendar (`Sun–Sat` Real-Date Grid)**:
  - Tracks your real-world calendar habit: click any date to inspect how many sessions, minutes, WPM, and daily tasks you completed on that date.
- **+100 XP Daily Mastery Bonus**:
  - Completing all 3 tasks for a scheduled day awards an automatic **+100 XP bonus** toward your typist level!
